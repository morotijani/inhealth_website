About Inhealth Medical Solutions

Advisory that stays close to how care actually gets delivered.

We're a Ghanaian healthcare and medical consultancy — built to sit between good policy and good practice, and make sure facilities, businesses and public programmes have what they need to deliver care well.

Why Inhealth exists

[Placeholder — client input needed] Inhealth Medical Solutions was founded to close a gap we kept seeing across Ghana's health sector: strong clinical intent, held back by weak systems — facilities without the right equipment, staff without structured training, programmes without the advisory support to run well.
